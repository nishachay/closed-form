      About

      Why Closed Form exists

      AI now writes research mathematics faster than people can read it. We translate it.

      Each entry explains one result in plain words. It covers what the result proves, who asked the question first, why it matters, what it could lead to and what it shows about AI. We write in a controlled, simple English based on ASD-STE100.

      Trust labels

      Formal proof listed
      At least one paper in the result has a main theorem listed in the repository’s Lean catalogue. A listing is not the same as an independent check.
      Partly formalized
      The result links to Lean notes, but no main theorem is listed as formally proved.
      Claimed, not checked
      Only the written paper exists. It could contain errors.

      What we don’t do

      We don’t call a result true because a company published it. Many mathematicians, including Fields medalists, have asked for careful review of AI results, and we agree. Every entry has a hype check and a link to the original paper.

      How entries are made

      An AI reader drafts each entry from the paper’s source. A second, separate AI checks every claim against the paper. A person reviews the draft before it gets published.
